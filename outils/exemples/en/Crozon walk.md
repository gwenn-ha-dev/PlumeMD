# Walking the Crozon Peninsula

Four days on the *GR 34* coastal path, from **Le Fret** to **Morgat**, in late September. Roughly 70 km of cliff path with the sea on the right the whole way. Notes for next time, and for anyone who asks.

## The route

1. Le Fret → Roscanvel — about 17 km, gentle: oyster beds and the Brest roadstead
2. Roscanvel → Camaret-sur-Mer — about 19 km, by the Pointe des Espagnols
3. Camaret → Kerloc'h — about 15 km, Pen-Hir and the *Tas de Pois*; the hardest climbs
4. Kerloc'h → Morgat — about 18 km, round the Cap de la Chèvre

> The wind turned on the third morning. We waited an hour at Pen-Hir for the squall to pass, and it was worth it: the Tas de Pois came out of the rain one stack at a time.

## What to pack

- A light rain shell — it rains *and* shines, often within the hour
- Two litres of water: there is little between Camaret and Kerloc'h
- The tide times for Morgat, to cross the beach at low water
- Cash for the crêperie in Camaret

## Getting there

Trains run to Brest. In summer a ferry crosses the roadstead to Le Fret; out of season, the bus to Crozon is the fallback. The path itself is waymarked in white and red — see [GR 34](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GR_34).
